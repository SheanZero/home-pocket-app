Home Pocket is a local-first household budgeting app. No sign-in or demo account is required. On first launch, tap “Get started,” complete Initial Setup with any nickname, language, and currency, and optionally skip app-lock setup. The core ledger, analytics, shopping list, backup, and settings features are then available on one device.

Family Sync is optional and requires two app installations. On device A, open Settings > Family > Create a new family and generate an invite code. On device B, open the same section, choose Join family, enter the six-digit code, and confirm. Approve the request on device A. Shared data is encrypted on device and transferred through our zero-knowledge relay at https://sync.happypocket.app. The relay temporarily stores opaque encrypted messages until acknowledgement or expiry; it cannot decrypt ledger contents. The backend will remain available during review. A two-device demonstration video is attached if requested.

Voice entry first requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions. The app prefers on-device recognition. If the device cannot provide on-device recognition, a user-controlled fallback may use the system network speech-recognition service. No audio is stored by the developer.

The app uses native APNs for optional family notifications. It also contacts public exchange-rate providers when the user requests a currency conversion; no transaction amount, note, or identity is included in that rate request.

The “Support development” row in Settings opens an external contact/support page. It is optional, unlocks no app functionality, and does not sell digital goods or services. The app has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, advertising, or behavioral tracking.

OCR/receipt scanning is intentionally disabled in this release and is not represented in the store metadata or screenshots.
