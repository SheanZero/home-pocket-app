Home Pocket is a local-first family budgeting app that makes room for both everyday needs and the spending that genuinely brings you joy.

Separate expenses into Daily and Joy ledgers. Along with the amount, record categories, notes, and satisfaction so you can understand what supported your household and what made life feel better.

KEY FEATURES
• Daily and Joy dual-ledger budgeting
• Fast manual entry and voice entry
• Categories, notes, and satisfaction for each transaction
• Monthly trends, category breakdowns, and Joy reflections
• Multiple currencies with the applied exchange rate saved
• Shared family shopping lists plus private personal lists
• Encrypted backup and restore
• Face ID or passcode app lock
• Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and English

SHARE ONLY WHAT YOU CHOOSE
Create a family group with an invite code and sync shared ledgers and shopping items. Sync content is encrypted on device and delivered through a zero-knowledge relay service. Private ledgers and private shopping items stay outside the family-sharing flow.

PRIVACY AT THE CENTER
Budget data is encrypted on your device and is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking. Network communication needed for family sync, notifications, and exchange rates is explained in the privacy policy.

Budgeting does not have to be only about restriction. Home Pocket helps you and your family understand what matters in everyday life.
