<interfaces>
- ExportBackupUseCase.execute accepts bookId, password, optional deviceId/appVersion, and an injected outputDirectory; it snapshots through UnitOfWork and atomically publishes a v2 file.
- ImportBackupUseCase.execute accepts File/password and owns decrypt, decompress, validate, one transaction, and compensation.
- RestoreBackupUseCase.execute wraps the importer with suspend/reset/resume sync callbacks.
- ClearAllDataUseCase is constructed from injected journal, sync, database, owned-file, secure-data, settings, and memory steps.
- BackupCryptoService accepts HPB version 2 by magic/version and rejects unsupported versions/KDF bounds with typed failures.
</interfaces>
