## Get help

Start with the [frequently asked questions]({{< relref "faq.md" >}}) and [privacy design]({{< relref "privacy.md" >}}) for supported platforms, family-sharing boundaries, and data handling.

## Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Happy Pocket is open source. You can use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SheanZero/home-pocket-app/issues), but do not include real names, transactions, photos, keys, invitation codes, or other sensitive information.

## Privacy and data deletion

Plaintext ledger data is primarily stored on your device and can be removed from the app settings. The family-sync relay handles encrypted messages and necessary operational metadata. A verified operator contact and privacy-request process will be added here before public release.
